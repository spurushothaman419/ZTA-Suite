# RACI Matrix Template - Zero Trust Assessment

## What is RACI?
- **R** = Responsible (Does the work)
- **A** = Accountable (Final authority, signs off)
- **C** = Consulted (Provides input, two-way communication)
- **I** = Informed (Kept in the loop, one-way communication)

## Customization Instructions

1. **Add/Remove Roles**: Modify columns to match your organization's structure
2. **Add/Remove Activities**: Add rows for specific tasks relevant to your assessment
3. **Update Assignments**: Change R/A/C/I based on your team's responsibilities

---

## Best Practices

✅ **Each activity should have exactly ONE 'A' (Accountable)**  
✅ At least one 'R' (Responsible) per activity  
✅ Minimize the number of 'C' (Consulted) to avoid bottlenecks  
✅ Use 'I' (Informed) liberally to keep stakeholders aware
